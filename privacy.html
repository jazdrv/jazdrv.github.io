Privacy policy

This application is a personal, single-user set of command-line tools operated by its owner solely to access the owner's own Google Calendar and Google Contacts data. The same OAuth client is used by gcalcli for calendar access and by a small personal script for contacts.

  • It requests two Google scopes: Calendar, in order to read and write the owner's calendar events, and Contacts, in order to read and maintain the owner's own contact entries.
  • Calendar and contact data is used only on the owner's own devices and is not transmitted to, stored by, or shared with any third party.
  • No data is sold, used for advertising, or used to train models.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's device and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
