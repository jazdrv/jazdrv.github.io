Privacy policy

These are personal, single-user command-line utilities operated by their owner to access the owner's own account data. They have no other users.

  • Access is limited to the owner's own calendar and contact entries, and is used only to read and maintain them.
  • Data is used only on the owner's own devices. It is not transmitted to, stored by, or shared with any third party.
  • No data is sold, used for advertising, or used to train models.
  • Authorisation tokens are held locally on the owner's device and may be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
