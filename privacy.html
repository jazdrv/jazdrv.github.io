Privacy policy

This application is a personal, single-user installation of gcalcli operated by its owner solely to access the owner's own Google Calendar data.

  • It requests the Google Calendar scope only, in order to read and write calendar events.
  • Calendar data is used only on the owner's own devices and is not transmitted to, stored by, or shared with any third party.
  • No data is sold, used for advertising, or used to train models.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's device and can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This application's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
