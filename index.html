jazdrv

Personal command-line tools. Single user. Not a product.

This page exists to carry the homepage and privacy-policy URLs that Google requires before a personal OAuth application can be published, so that its access tokens do not expire every seven days.

What uses it

  • gcalcli — reads and writes the owner's own Google Calendar from the terminal.
  • gcontacts — a small personal script that maintains the owner's own Google Contacts. It shares the same OAuth client.

Neither is offered to anyone else, and neither has any other users.

Privacy

Privacy policy — what is accessed, where it stays, and how to revoke it.

These two pages are load-bearing: if they stop resolving, Google can drop the application out of production and the seven-day token expiry returns.
